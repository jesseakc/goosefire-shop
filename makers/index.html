The artist

Alesha

Alesha is the artist behind Goose Fire Ceramics. She builds the forms, decides what each piece should look like, and does the carving and glazing that makes them hers.

She also holds a full-time office job. Ceramics is what she is building toward.

A studio put together by hand

Most of this studio arrived through Facebook Marketplace. The kiln cost a hundred dollars. The wheel was inexpensive. The rest came piece by piece, until there was enough of it to work in.

She is continually on the hunt for slipcasting moulds, and she gives each one her own spin.

Moulds, and what she does with them

Alesha is always on the lookout for slipcasting moulds. She buys them, borrows ideas from them, and then puts them through something more than the mould intended.

The moulds she makes here are cast in plaster from printed forms. The ornament designs we use come from Old Forge Creations, who publish 3D files for exactly this kind of piece. Casting, trimming, carving and glazing are all done in this studio.

Geese, pigs, and a lot of evenings

Give Alesha a goose and she will give it a personality. The pigs get the most attention and the least seriousness. She loves the art itself, geese and pigs included.

She works a full-time office job. Making the studio her full-time life is the dream.

Jesse

Jesse is Alesha's partner. He helps run the studio, shoots the video, and makes ceramic ornaments of his own.
