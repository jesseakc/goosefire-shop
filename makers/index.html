The artist

Alesha

She is curious about the art itself, and about the moulds it comes in. Give her a goose and she will give it a personality.

A studio put together by hand

Most of this studio arrived through Facebook Marketplace. The kiln cost a hundred dollars. The wheel was inexpensive. The rest came piece by piece, until there was enough of it to work in.

What she does to a mould

Every piece gets her own spin

She is continually on the hunt for slipcasting moulds, and what she does next is the interesting part. A cast form can be left plain, or it can be carved, spotted, given ears, or given an expression.

Some of the cast work leaves the mould as it came out. Some of it is worked and fired again before it ever sees glaze.

None of the forms she uses are hers by design, and she says so plainly. The ornament designs come from Old Forge Creations, who publish 3D files for exactly this kind of piece. The plaster moulds are made here, in the studio.

Geese, pigs, and a lot of evenings

She works an office job through the week and spends a great many hours in the studio around it. Making this her full-time life is the dream.
