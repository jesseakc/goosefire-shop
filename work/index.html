The work

Three things we spend our time on. Nothing here is for sale yet — this page is so you can see what the studio actually makes.

Carved sculpture

Swan and pumpkin

Tooled while soft. The feather and face texture is cut, not moulded.

Out of the kiln

Glaze firing, cone 6 oxidation. No two pieces come out the same colour.

Ceramic ornaments

The moulds

Each mould is carved by hand from a leaf, then used to slipcast ornaments.

See how a piece is made

Geese, and pigs with opinions

Pig in hand

Alesha casts a form, then keeps going: adding, removing, and giving it an expression the mould never had in mind.

Waiting for the firing

A full kiln of bisque, packed on shelves with posts between them so nothing touches. Everything gets two firings before it is finished.

Some pieces here are wheel-thrown, some slipcast, and some carved or assembled from cast parts. Which one a piece is matters less than who decided what it should be.

Our ornament mould designs are the work of Old Forge Creations. Alesha casts the plaster, pours every ornament, and does all the trimming and glazing herself — which is why no two come out the same.
