The work

Ornaments, carved forms, and pigs

Pots, hand-worked ornaments for the tree, and pigs with personality. The pigs and ornaments are slipcast, so the clay starts liquid and pours into a plaster mould. Alesha finds the mould, then decides what the piece should be.
