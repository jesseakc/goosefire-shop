Slipcast

Ornaments, and why no two are the same

The mould designs are the work of Old Forge Creations, and we are grateful for them. Alesha casts the plaster, pours every ornament, and does all the trimming and glazing herself — which is why no two come out the same.

The pigs

Given a personality the mould never had in mind

Give Alesha a goose and she will give it a personality. Cast forms get modified, carved further, and glazed until they stop being quite what the mould intended.

Some pieces here are wheel-thrown, some slipcast, and some carved or assembled from cast parts. Which one a piece is matters less than who decided what it should be.
