The work

Things with a bit of personality

Ceramic ornaments, carved forms, and a small number of slipcast pigs. A few are finished, and others are further along the way.

The studio

A studio she put together herself

Most of it came off Facebook Marketplace. The kiln she got for a hundred dollars. A pottery wheel that cost barely more than a good dinner. Everything else turned up the same way, a piece at a time.

Alesha also has a full-time office job. Ceramics is what she is building toward, one firing at a time.

At the wheel

Carving, glazing, and watching what the kiln does

Most pieces start as liquid clay poured into a plaster mould. Once the form is out and trimmed, it gets worked by hand: a texture cut into the surface, or an extra detail added that the mould never asked for.

Then two firings, and whatever the glaze decides to do. That is a large part of why no two pieces end up looking quite the same.
