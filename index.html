The artist

Alesha

She built this studio one piece at a time, mostly through Facebook Marketplace. The kiln cost a hundred dollars. The wheel was inexpensive. What she could not buy, she made.

She works an office job through the week and spends a great many hours in the studio around it, late nights included. She is endlessly on the hunt for slipcasting moulds, and she gives nearly every one of them her own spin.

Making this her full-time life is the dream. Every piece that comes out of the kiln is a step toward it.

Character in clay

Give her a mould and she will give it an opinion

Every piece starts as a form somebody else made. What happens between the mould and the kiln is where it becomes something you won't find twice.

The winged pig is the most honest example: the mould gives a pig, and the bench adds wings, ears, and an expression nobody ordered.

Supporting the studio

Jesse

Jesse is Alesha’s partner. He helps keep the studio running, and he makes ceramic ornaments of his own, the kind that go on a Christmas tree.
