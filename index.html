The work

Things with a bit of personality

Ceramic ornaments, carved forms, and a small number of slipcast pigs. Some are finished, some are still on the way.

The studio

A studio she put together herself

Most of it came off Facebook Marketplace. The kiln she got for a hundred dollars. A pottery wheel that cost barely more than a good dinner. Everything else turned up the same way, a piece at a time.

Alesha also has a full-time office job. Ceramics is what she is building toward, one firing at a time.

At the wheel

Carving, glazing, and watching what the kiln does

Most pieces start as liquid clay poured into a plaster mould. Once the form is out and trimmed, it gets worked by hand: a texture cut into the surface, a leaf pressed in, or an extra detail added that the mould never asked for.

Then two firings, and whatever the glaze decides to do. Alesha keeps her eye on the glaze tests, which is part of why the work varies so much from piece to piece.
