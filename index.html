Selected work

Geese, pigs, and pots with a mind of their own

Most of what leaves this studio is slipcast: liquid clay poured into a plaster mould, then trimmed, glazed, and fired twice. Alesha finds the mould and decides what the piece should be.

The artist

Alesha

She built this studio one piece at a time, mostly through Facebook Marketplace. The kiln cost a hundred dollars. The wheel was inexpensive. What she could not buy, she made.

She works an office job through the week and spends a great many hours in the studio around it, late nights included. She is endlessly on the hunt for slipcasting moulds, and she gives nearly every one of them her own spin.

Making this her full-time life is the dream. Every piece that comes out of the kiln is a step toward it.
